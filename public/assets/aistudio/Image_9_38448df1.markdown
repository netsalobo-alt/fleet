## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, executive telemetry atmosphere for enterprise fleet operations. Blending corporate discipline with connected-telecom vitality, the aesthetic balances the authority of deep naval corporate hues with energetic telemetry highlights. 

The visual style is **Corporate Modern with High-Density Precision**:
- **Tone & Mood:** Command-ready, proactive, clear, and reassuringly precise.
- **Visual Tenets:** High contrast between deep corporate navies and vibrant telemetry accents; tactile, airy surfaces over crisp micro-borders; instant cognitive triage through semantic alert accents.
- **Context of Use:** Primarily field managers, mobile dispatchers, and fleet executives requiring instant clarity under varying daylight environments on mobile viewports.

## Colors

The palette leverages a structured semantic hierarchy rooted in telecommunications and operational monitoring:

- **Primary (`#0F2744`):** Deep Navy Telecom. Anchors navigation headers, executive metric summaries, primary headings, and high-emphasis controls.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`):** Vibrant Telecom Blue. Directs primary calls to action, active navigation tabs, interactive links, and ongoing tracking sessions.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4`):** Telemetry & Connectivity Cyan. Serves as the real-time heartbeat tokenâ€”employed for live GPS pings, sync indicators (e.g., Chatwoot status), and dynamic data pulses.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Canvas / Background: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Surface Substrate: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - Pure Surface Card: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border Subdued: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
  - Secondary Text / Icons: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
  - Body / Data Text: `#1E293B` (Slate 800)
- **Operational & Status Semantics:**
  - **Success / On-Schedule:** `#10B981` (Telemetry green for active vehicles, valid inspections).
  - **Warning / Review Required:** `#F59E0B` (Amber for mileage thresholds, upcoming preventive maintenance).
  - **Critical / Immediate Action:** `#EF4444` (Crimson for overdue tax/IPVA, telemetry loss, critical engine errors).

## Typography

Typography centers exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, capitalizing on its geometric clarity, tall x-height, and contemporary executive finish:

- **Metrics & Numbers:** Financial and mileage values utilize `metric-number` with tabular numeric alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifts during live data re-renders.
- **Labels & Badges:** Upper-tier metadata badges rely on `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to ensure legibility on low-contrast tinted backgrounds.
- **Mobile Readability:** Display sizes are capped at 32px to ensure vehicle identifiers, license plates, and status ribbons fit comfortably on compact screens without breaking line heights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a mobile-first, single-column fluid container with fixed bounds for tablet and desktop viewports:

- **Grid Model:** Mobile leverages an edge-to-edge flexible layout within safe-area insets. Screen margins default to `1rem` (16px), allowing cards to maximize lateral scan area while maintaining visual breathability.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (fluid, single column, bottom sheet interactions, sticky bottom quick-action bar).
